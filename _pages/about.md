I am an M.Sc. student in Computer Science at ETH Zurich. My research focuses on the **computational modeling and analysis of biological networks** and the **interpretability and explainability of biomedical models**.

Currently, I am a Visiting Graduate Researcher in the [Martin Zhang Lab](https://mzhanglab.github.io/) at Carnegie Mellon University, advised by Prof. Martin Jinye Zhang, where I integrate cell type information into KGWAS, a knowledge graph-based machine learning model for genome-wide association studies. At ETH Zurich, I work with Prof. Gunnar Rätsch in the [Biomedical Informatics Group](https://bmi.inf.ethz.ch/) on training sparse autoencoders to interpret and steer clinical time-series foundation models.

Previously, I received a B.Sc. in Mathematics (Honors) and a B.Sc. in Computer Science (Departmental Honors) from the University of Texas at Austin, where I worked with [Prof. Can Cenik](https://ceniklab.github.io/) on translation efficiency covariation networks. I also spent a summer at the [Physiological Laboratories](https://phyzlab.org/) at Dell Medical School with [Prof. Joshua Chang](https://jtkchang.github.io/), evaluating deep learning models for heart rate forecasting in preterm infants.
